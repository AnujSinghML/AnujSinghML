## 🧭 How I make product decisions

- **Start where the pain costs money.** Collections began as one clinic's complaint that its collections agency wasn't recovering balances. I made the business case to the practice manager, billing manager and lead physician, and it became a product running in 40 clinics.
- **Keep humans in the loop where trust matters.** In small practices, doctors know their patients personally. So I shipped firmer and softer reminder sequences, required staff approval before escalation, and chose not to auto-charge stored cards.
- **Kill the approach that doesn't scale, even if it works.** RPA took 7 hours for 5,000 patients. I replaced it with an EHR export joined with FHIR data.
- **Measure outcomes, not features.** I ran A/B tests on reminder timing and cadence, tracked payment-link clicks, and set up conversion funnels for referrals.
